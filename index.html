About Crest Dreamfield Academy

Crest Dreamfield Academy started with a dream—to create a learning experience where learning begins with wonder. That dream first came to life through our learning clubs in China, then continued online as we reached more families beyond the classroom. Now, we’re bringing that same vision to San Pascual, Batangas, Philippines. At CREST, children are invited to explore, imagine, and grow through storybook-inspired learning. Along the way, they can join Ellie, our cute little elephant friend who loves to wonder, discover, and take children on adventures where they learn alongside him.

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages do you accept? +
We welcome learners from 1 to 16 years old, with programs designed to meet different developmental stages and learning needs.
How do I enroll my child? +
Send us a message through Facebook Messenger or email to request enrollment guidance and the current requirements.
What programs are available? +
We currently offer:

• Wonder & Play (Ages 1–16)
• Crest Tutorials
• Stay & Play (Child Care Services)

Visit our Programs page to learn more about each program.
How much is tuition? +
For current tuition rates, enrollment fees, and other payment details, please message Crest Dreamfield Academy using the Message Us on Messenger button on our homepage. Our team will be happy to provide the latest tuition information and assist you with enrollment.
When will the Academy open? +
Our soft opening was held on August 22, 2026, and enrollment is now open. Regular classes will officially begin on September 1, 2026.

We are excited to welcome our Little Dreamers and begin this wonderful learning adventure together!
Are late enrollees accepted? +
Yes! Late enrollment is accepted and we encourage families who are interested to join us even after the program has started.

For late enrollees, the applicable fees will cover the month they join through the end of the program. Please note that these payments are non-refundable. All payment terms, conditions, and other important details are outlined in the enrollment contract.

For example, if a learner enrolls during the second week of September, they will still need to pay the applicable fee for the entire month of September. Alternatively, they may choose to wait and begin with the October class.

If you would like to join early but are not yet ready to officially commit as a member, you may also register as a Non-Member. You can participate by paying the applicable Non-Member fee and eventually enroll as a regular member in the exact month you wish to begin your membership.

For specific enrollment and payment details, please message the Academy through the Message Us on Messenger button on the homepage.
Can I enroll as a Non-Member? +
Yes! You may sign up as a Non-Member if you are interested in joining our learning activities but are not yet ready to commit to the full program.

Non-Members are welcome to participate; however, Non-Member fees and charges are different from Member rates.

If you are considering becoming a member later, you may eventually enroll as a Member in the exact month you wish to officially begin your membership.

For current Non-Member rates, available activities, and other details, please message Crest Dreamfield Academy using the Message Us on Messenger button on the homepage.
When will the FREE Trial Class Schedule be announced? +
The FREE Trial Class Schedule will be announced soon. Please follow our Facebook page or contact us for updates.
How do I book a trial class? +
You can register for a FREE trial class through our online registration form.

After submitting the form, our team will contact you to confirm your preferred schedule and provide the next steps.
What is the Student Portal? +
The Student Portal is a private area where enrolled students can view their profile, attendance, badges, journal entries, and teacher feedback.
How do students log in to the portal? +
Use the username and password provided by the Academy. If your credentials do not work, contact the Academy instead of sharing them publicly.
Can parents access the Student Portal? +
Parents or guardians may request access details from the Academy. Do not share student passwords in public messages.
How is attendance displayed? +
After login, the dashboard displays the attendance percentage and any session summary supplied by the Academy records.
Where can I see my child’s journal entries? +
Journal entries appear on the dashboard after login, showing dates, summaries, photos when available, and teacher feedback. The journal is updated regularly and may reset as new physical journal entries are collected, keeping only the latest learning moments visible.
Where can I see badges? +
Badges assigned to the student appear in the My Badges panel on the dashboard.
Where can I find class schedules? +
Schedules will be communicated by the Academy through the official contact channels and updated announcements.
How can I contact Crest Dreamfield Academy? +
Use the Message Us on Facebook button to contact the Academy for assistance.
